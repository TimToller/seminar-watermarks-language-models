\begin{frame}{Outlook \& Personal opinion}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item The \emph{Kuditipudi et al.}\ watermark described in this presentation works only if the \textbf{LM Provider} and the \textbf{detector} work together. This is unreliable once users move to open-source LMs or switch providers.
        \item Beyond scrubbing a watermark, we may want to \emph{prove} human authorship or certify other attributes, each needing a different embed/detect design and careful control of false positives/negatives.
        \item There are many different ideas and methods to choose from, it's still not quite clear which approach is best long-term.
        \item Research should target provider-agnostic, robust schemes. Adoption could arrive through regulation or market incentives.
        \item Major LMs may already watermark their text and we wouldn't be able to tell.
    \end{itemize}
\end{frame}
