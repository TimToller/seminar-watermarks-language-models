\begin{frame}{Step 3: Checking the watermark}

    To reiterate the p-values we obtained, we have:

    \begin{center}

        \begin{tabular}{ccc}
            \textbf{Plain/Control} & \textbf{Watermarked} & \textbf{Edited} \\
            \hline
            p=0.8020               & p=0.0099             & p=0.0099
        \end{tabular}
    \end{center}

    \bigskip

    A text is considered watermarked if \textbf{p < 0.01}.

    As expected, the control text is not watermarked, while the watermarked and edited texts are both watermarked.
